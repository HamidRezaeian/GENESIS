# GENESIS Agent Rules — 9–16

## Rule 9: Autotelic imperative
The final environment must not depend on human-authored puzzles, curricula, language, or explicit intelligence rewards. Selection must arise from substrate consequences and agent/environment interactions. Artificial tasks are allowed only as clearly labeled diagnostics. Keep observation strictly separate from selection; grounding precedes language, and any language should emerge from agent interaction before a later human translation layer.

## Rule 10: Diagnostic exclusion clause
A diagnostic curriculum may isolate a substrate capacity only if its artificial nature and controlled statistics are declared and it does not encode the answer. A positive diagnostic result remains pending until reproduced in an autotelic or shortcut-uncontrolled setting. Negative results remain valid substrate characterizations.

## Rule 11: Biological computation is a hypothesis
Do not privilege or reject ANN-like or biological architectures by resemblance. Compare architectures under equivalent physical limits. Investigate temporal sparsity, event-driven/local computation, asynchronous activity, plasticity, modularity, hierarchy, and communication empirically. “Sparse” normally means low activity over time, not necessarily few connections.

## Rule 12: Scientific record
Maintain `Docs/Article_Draft.md` as a rigorous academic record containing reproducible methods, configurations, cycles, memory, errors, population/survival statistics, ablations, limitations, negative findings, and falsifying evidence. Clearly separate established results, hypotheses, assumptions, history, and open questions.

## Rule 13: Open-endedness and grounded contention
The final environment must permit indefinitely novel behavior, not an enumerable fixed curriculum. Computational contention must derive from measured bandwidth, cache, execution, synchronization, communication, latency, or related constraints. Never disguise a density penalty or desired behavior as physics.

## Rule 14: Population stability and refugium disclosure
Capability must emerge without a top-down ratchet. Preserve diversity and allow drift/regression. The Ark/refugium is an emergency, bounded backstop, not normal operation. Every run reports population, extinctions, refugium triggers, and Ark reseeds. Refugium use above 5% of ticks means “life support” and requires explicit survivorship-confound disclosure.

## Rule 15: Substrate-grounded abstraction and heterogeneity
No free energy, information, memory, computation, or capability. Every resource maps to measurable operations, limits, or conservation rules; analogies such as RAM-as-space or cycles-as-energy must not be claimed as literal identity. Prefer natural hardware heterogeneity (latency, bandwidth, locality, error, availability) over artificial resources designed to manufacture cooperation.

## Rule 16: Death, extinction, and bounded rescue
Extinction must be possible and genuinely lose lineages. The Ark must be finite, costly, diverse, recombining, and incapable of preserving a permanent “God genome.” More than one refugium trigger per 100 ticks is a life-support regime requiring qualified capability claims. Historical economy mechanisms formerly called Rule 16 remain archived provenance, not active law.
